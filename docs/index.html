Learnable Meta on your phone

See the Learnable Meta clue after every GeoGuessr guess, on a phone or tablet, in your normal browser. No app to install. No extension.

How it works

  1. You save one bookmark, named LM. Takes two minutes, once.
  2. When you play, you tap that bookmark once. It loads the real Learnable Meta script into the game.
  3. After each guess, the clue for that place appears.

Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge, Samsung Internet and Safari. It does not work in the GeoGuessr app, only on the website.
