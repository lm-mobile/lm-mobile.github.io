Learnable Meta on your phone

See the clue after every GeoGuessr guess, on a phone or tablet, in your normal browser. No app to install. No extension.

I have the LM bookmark, let's play
Every new map, tap LM again. The bookmark only lasts for the game you are in. Start a new map or reload the page, and you need to tap it once more.

Set up once. Pick your phone.

AndroidChrome, Brave, Edge or Samsung Internet. Five steps, two minutes. iPhone or iPadSafari. Four steps, two minutes.

How it works

  1. You save one bookmark, named LM. Once.
  2. In a game, you tap that bookmark. It loads the real Learnable Meta script into the page.
  3. After each guess, the clue for that place appears. New map? Tap LM again.

Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge, Samsung Internet and Safari. It does not work in the GeoGuessr app, only on the website.
