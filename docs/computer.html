Computer: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox or Safari once

On a computer you can also just install Tampermonkey and the official script. Use this page if you would rather not, or to create the bookmark that syncs to your phone.

1

Show the bookmarks bar

Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows, Linux) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac). A bar with your bookmarks appears under the address bar.

2

Drag this button onto the bar

Hold the mouse button down on it, move it onto the bookmarks bar, and let go: LM

Cannot drag? Right-click the bookmarks bar, choose Add page (or Add bookmark), name it LM, and paste this into the address field:

Show the link
3

Test it

Open www.geoguessr.com and click LM on the bookmarks bar. A message "Learnable Meta 0.96 loaded" appears at the bottom, plus a round LM button. Now go to Play.

4

Send it to your phone (Chrome)

If Chrome on the computer and Chrome on your Android phone are signed in to the same Google account, the LM bookmark appears on the phone by itself. Then follow step 5 on the Android page.
