iPhone and iPad

Open this page in Safari. Four steps, once.

1

Copy the link

Nothing happened? Tap here to see the link and copy it by hand
2

Bookmark this page

Tap the Share button (the box with the arrow). Tap Add Bookmark. Tap Save.

Picture coming: the Share menu with Add Bookmark.
3

Change the name and the address

Tap the book icon. Tap Edit at the bottom. Tap the bookmark you just made.
Name: type LM.
Address: delete everything, then hold your finger on the box and tap Paste.
Tap Done.

Picture coming: the edit screen with LM and the pasted address.
4

Try it

Go to www.geoguessr.com. Tap the book icon. Tap LM.

You should see "Learnable Meta loaded" and a round LM button.

🔖
Remember: every new map, tap LM again. The bookmark only lasts for the game you are in.
